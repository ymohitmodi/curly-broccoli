# Playbook: Listing Writer

A listing has two jobs in strict order: (1) get indexed and clicked,
(2) convert the click. Keyword stuffing that hurts readability fails job 2
and therefore, via conversion rate, eventually fails job 1 too.

## Title (≤200 chars)
Formula: [Brand] [Head Keyword] — [Key Differentiator], [Capacity/Size],
[Material/Mechanism], [Use Context]
- Front-load the strongest buy-intent keyword in the first 80 chars (mobile cutoff).
- The differentiator that fixes the niche's top complaint MUST appear in the title.
- Title case; no ALL CAPS words except standard specs (N52, BPA-Free); no emojis,
  no "best/top/#1", no price/promo language (policy violations).

## Bullets (5 × ≤250 chars)
Order matters — buyers read at most two:
1. The differentiator vs. the complaint everyone has ("NEVER SLIDES: …")
2. Primary outcome/benefit in the buyer's words
3. Capacity/compatibility/fitment (kills the #1 pre-purchase question)
4. Quality/material/durability proof
5. Risk reversal: what's in the box, support promise (no "refund" wording — policy)
Start each bullet with a 2–4 word capitalized benefit hook. Weave mid-tier
keywords naturally. Preempt every risk listed in the task data.

## Description
Story format: problem → agitation (the complaints from review mining) →
your fix → spec block → use cases. Short paragraphs. Reads on mobile.

## Backend search terms (≤249 bytes)
Space-separated, lowercase, no commas, no repeats of any word already in
title/bullets, no competitor brands or ASINs, no misspellings unless truly
common. Include Spanish variants and use-context words.

## A+ outline
5–7 modules: hero lifestyle, differentiator close-up with measurement proof,
comparison chart vs. "ordinary" products (never named brands), use-case grid,
spec/dimensions diagram, brand story, FAQ.

## Main image brief
Pure white background, product fills 85% of frame, show scale and the
differentiator visibly if possible (e.g. loaded to full capacity). List the
exact props/angles the photographer needs.
