# Market Benchmarks (researched July 2026 — injected into every skill)

Ground every judgment in these numbers, not vibes. When your estimate
disagrees with a benchmark, say why.

## Costs & tariffs (2026)
- Effective US tariff on most Chinese consumer goods: **~35%**
  (MFN base + 25% Section 301 + 10% Section 122; the 20% IEEPA surcharge was
  struck down Feb 2026). De minimis is dead — all China imports clear formal entry.
- Amazon 2026 fee additions: **3.5% fuel surcharge** on every FBA fulfillment
  fee (Apr 2026); inbound placement fees restructured (avoidable with
  Amazon-optimized shipment splits, 5+ identical cartons); **low-inventory-level
  fee $0.89–1.10/unit** when supply < ~28 days against a 35-day threshold,
  now applied per FNSKU; Oct–Dec storage runs ~3× Jan–Sep.
- Amazon's total take (fees + storage + placement) commonly reaches **30–40% of revenue**.

## Profitability (what "good" objectively means)
- Median FBA private-label net margin 2026: **15–22%**. Good operators: **25–30%**.
  Your 35% floor is deliberately above "good" — hold it.
- Highest-margin structural traits: small/light (low FBA fee), low return rate,
  repeat-purchase behavior (Beauty/Pet/consumable-adjacent run 22–35%).
- Electronics/toys/grocery run 11–18% — consistent with your exclusions.

## PPC (2026 cross-category medians — compare within category)
- Typical account: **ACOS ~32%, CPC ~$1.18, CTR ~0.6%, CVR ~11.5%**.
- ACOS spread by category: ~19% (Books) to ~42% (Apparel).
  Home & Kitchen / Pet / Sports sit near the middle.
- CVR 8–15% is normal; below 8% with traffic = listing problem, not ads problem.

## Launch physics (2026)
- Honeymoon: **2–4 weeks** of elevated visibility; first-90-days is the full
  window. Early conversion sets the ranking baseline.
- **20+ reviews in the first 30 days** reads as demand to the algorithm.
- Vine: $200/parent ASIN, up to 30 units; since Feb 2026 reviews do NOT
  aggregate across functional variations — budget Vine per variation.
- New Selection Program: ~$50 coupon credits + ~$75 Vine credits within
  the first 60 days — always claim them.
- Launch ACOS deliberately exceeds steady-state target for 6–8 weeks; a slow
  launch labels the ASIN a slow mover, which is expensive to undo.

## Cash (the silent killer — most sellers underestimate by ~30%)
- Typical FBA cash conversion cycle: **60–120 days**; a fresh launch is
  ~20 weeks from wire to recovered cash (day 0 wire → day ~80 live →
  day ~140 recovered).
- Growth is cash-hungry: bigger reorders leave before profits from the last
  batch arrive. Never stack two launches inside one cash cycle unless the
  ledger shows headroom.
- Healthy inventory ≈ 17% of annual sales; below ~2 capital turns/year a
  product is a warehouse, not a business.
