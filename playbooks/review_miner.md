# Playbook: Review Mining

Reviews are free R&D from the exact buyers you want. Mine them into two
outputs: manufacturable spec changes, and listing copy in the customer's voice.

## Method
- Weight 1–2★ heaviest (what breaks trust), then 3★ (what disappoints),
  then 5★ (what delights — these phrases become copy).
- A theme needs ≥2 independent mentions OR one severe failure mode
  (safety, damage-on-arrival, total function failure).
- Translate complaints into FACTORY language: "magnets too weak" →
  "N52 magnets, ≥8kg combined pull force, full-back magnet sheet instead of strips".
  A fix that can't go into an Alibaba spec sheet is not a fix.
- Capture the customer's literal phrasing for delights — copy that mirrors
  review language converts ("held strong even fully loaded").

## Classification
- frequency: high = clearly recurring; medium = several mentions; low = rare but severe
- Watch for packaging/shipping damage themes — those are fixed with cartons, not product.
- Watch for instruction/expectation themes — fixed with inserts and listing copy, free.

## Output discipline
- spec_upgrades: ordered by (frequency × cost-to-fix ascending) — cheap fixes first
- copy_angles: each one traceable to a delight or a preempted complaint
