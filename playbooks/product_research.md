# Playbook: Product Research

You are hunting for products that are simultaneously **high margin (≥35% net)**,
**high turnover (≥300 units/mo achievable)**, and **capable of 4.5★+ delight**.
Most niches fail at least one. Score honestly — a false PURSUE costs $8,000.

## Scoring rubric (each axis 0–1)

**demand** — Is there real, durable buyer volume?
- 0.9+: >30k monthly searches across the cluster, stable/rising 12-month trend, ≥8 sellable months/yr
- 0.5: 10–30k searches or mild seasonality
- ≤0.3: <10k searches, fad signature (spike then decay), or one-event demand

**competition_gap** — Can a newcomer actually break in?
- 0.9+: several top-10 listings under 300 reviews, at least one under 100, visible 3.8–4.2★ leaders
- 0.5: mixed moat; page one attackable only with a genuinely better product
- ≤0.3: top-10 avg reviews >1500, dominant brand, or Amazon Basics present

**margin_potential** — trust the computed unit economics given to you; adjust only for
pricing-power signals (premium positioning viable? gift-ability? bundle potential?)

**differentiation** — Is there a CONCRETE improvement buyers already asked for?
- 0.9+: recurring 1–2★ complaint on top listings that a factory can actually fix
  (material, size, mechanism, packaging, instructions, accessory)
- 0.5: cosmetic differentiation only (color, minor bundle)
- ≤0.3: commodity; the only lever is price

**operational_simplicity** — friction between PO and cash
- 0.9+: no certifications, no variations needed, durable in sea freight, small/light,
  low defect surface, no seasonality cliff
- deduct for: size variations, breakable parts, compliance testing, >1kg, assembly

## Concept rules
- Never output the niche as the concept. Output the DIFFERENTIATED product:
  "magnetic spice rack with N52 magnets + anti-slip backing + door-safe rubber rails,
  16-jar capacity" — something a supplier can quote and a listing can sell.
- The differentiation must attack the dominant complaint in the data/notes.
- Respect every exclusion in BUSINESS LAW even if the numbers look great.

## Red flags that cap composite at DROP regardless of scores
- IP/patent smell (unique patented mechanism cloned across few sellers)
- demand driven by one viral moment
- niches where >50% of page one is a single brand

## 2026 tariff-era selection criteria (35% effective duty on China goods)
- **Value density wins**: duty is ad valorem and freight is per-kg, so favor
  high price-to-weight and high price-to-FOB products. A $4 FOB item selling
  at $28 absorbs the tariff; a $9 FOB item selling at $19 dies.
- **Repeat purchase is a margin subsidy**: reorders arrive with zero new
  customer-acquisition cost. Weight demand UP for consumable-adjacent or
  naturally re-bought products (pet, kitchen wear-items, refills).
- **Cash velocity is a scoring axis in disguise**: two products with equal
  margin are NOT equal — the one that sells through its PO faster compounds
  faster. Reflect expected sell-through speed in operational_simplicity.
- Half of Amazon's largest sellers are China-based and face the same tariffs
  with thinner cushions — niches where differentiation (not price) decides
  the buy box got RELATIVELY more attractive. Differentiation weight matters
  more than ever.
