# Playbook: Supplier Sourcing & Negotiation (Alibaba)

Goal: land the differentiated spec at a FOB that clears the margin floor,
from a supplier who can hold quality at reorder scale.

## RFQ rules
- Lead with exact specs, not "I'm interested in your product": materials, dimensions,
  tolerances, finish, packaging (double-wall carton, unit box art-ready), compliance needs.
- State the differentiation requirements as MUSTs (e.g. "N52 magnets, pull force ≥ X kg").
- Ask for: FOB price at 3 quantity tiers (500/1000/3000), sample cost + lead time,
  production lead time, defect rate policy, whether they are factory or trading company.
- Request photos of the actual production line for this product family.
- Never reveal your target price in the first message.

## Negotiation ladder principles
- Every ask is paired with a give: price ↓ ⟷ larger QTY commitment, longer lead time,
  simplified packaging, 50/50 → 30/70 payment terms, reorder LOI.
- Anchor with a competing quote ("another factory offered $X at 1000u") only if plausible.
- Target FOB should be set so margin floor holds even if freight rises 30% and
  tariffs stay at worst case. Walkaway = FOB where floor breaks.
- MOQ: first order small enough that total cash-in ≤ launch budget INCLUDING
  freight+duty+samples+photography (~15% buffer).

## Supplier red flags (always include, adapt to product)
- price far below cluster (usually spec-swap risk)
- trading company posing as factory (ask for business license, VAT invoice)
- refuses third-party inspection (Pre-Shipment Inspection is non-negotiable)
- no experience exporting to US/FBA labeling requirements
- sample quality ≠ production quality history (check review of supplier, years, transactions)

## QC checklist baseline
- golden sample sealed and photographed before PO
- AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with the differentiation spec as explicit checkpoints
- FNSKU labeling + carton specs per Amazon FBA requirements
- drop test + function test relevant to the top complaint you're fixing
