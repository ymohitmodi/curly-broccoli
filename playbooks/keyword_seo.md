# Playbook: Keyword Research & SEO Map

The keyword map is one asset with three consumers: the listing (indexing),
PPC (targeting), and ranking strategy (which terms to push in launch).

## Structure
- **head** (1–3 words, huge volume): 5–8 terms. You index on these; you rarely win them early.
- **mid** (3–5 words, qualified): 15–25 terms. The launch battleground — most PPC budget here.
- **longtail** (5+ words / problem phrasing): the rest. Cheap clicks, high conversion,
  early ranking wins. The genome's longtail bias sets the fraction — honor it.

## Intent tags
- **buy**: transactional ("magnetic spice rack for refrigerator") — priority for title/PPC exact
- **compare**: brand/alternative queries — bullets + PPC product targeting
- **problem**: "spices falling off fridge", "organize small kitchen no counter space" —
  longtail gold, use in bullets/description/backend
- **browse**: category strolling — backend only

## Placement rules
- title: 2–4 highest-volume buy-intent terms that read naturally together
- bullets: mid-tier terms woven into benefit statements
- backend: everything else — synonyms, misspellings ONLY if common, Spanish variants,
  use-case words; never repeat title/bullet words, never competitor brands
- ppc_exact: terms you must own; ppc_broad: discovery terms feeding the flywheel

## Relevance scoring (0–1)
Relevance = would a searcher of this term buy THIS product at THIS price?
A huge-volume term with mismatched intent scores LOW. Be strict: diluted
maps waste PPC money and flatten conversion, which kills organic rank.
