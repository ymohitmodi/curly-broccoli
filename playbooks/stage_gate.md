# Playbook: The Validation Ladder (stage_gate)

The honest math of this business: nobody wins 90% of launches. Roughly a
third of sellers never reach profitability, and the failures cluster on
three causes — me-too products, cash-flow collapse, and ignoring data.
Elite operators don't out-pick the market; they out-STRUCTURE it: losers
die at $150 (a poll), $300 (a sample), or 25% of budget (an air test) —
never at 100%. The portfolio preserves 90%+ of capital even when half the
ideas die, and the survivors carry validated economics into the big bet.

Rules the engine enforces (config/pipeline.yaml — edit thresholds there):
- No candidate may consume more capital than its current gate allows.
- Kill criteria are written BEFORE money moves and travel with every
  approval request. Deciding when to quit while you're emotionally invested
  is how budgets die; pre-commitment is the antidote.
- Evidence is recorded by completing work orders — the factory writes the
  exact instructions; the human does the physical world.
- An air-freight micro-test buys INFORMATION, not margin: real CVR, real
  CPC, real reviews, ~5 weeks earlier than sea. Judge it as tuition, and
  read its results as the replacement for every estimate that preceded it.
